NexusPlay — purposeful motion audit

Audit of the motion system across the NexusPlay esports platform: the cinematic landing (GSAP + Lenis + CSS keyframes) and the productivity-grade role dashboards (Tailwind transitions, tokenized timing, press feedback). Weighted for its split context: dashboards judged by Emil (restraint & speed), landing polish by Jakub, hero/ring spectacle by Jhey.

OVERALL

This is a disciplined motion system wearing a cinematic coat. Timing is tokenized (120/180/300/600/900ms), easing is custom end-to-end, and the app-level reduced-motion kill-switch is genuinely complete — rare at any budget. What's missing is the second half of choreography: two of the three overlay surfaces (registration modal, mobile drawer) enter beautifully and then vanish with no exit at all, which is the exact "elements just disappear" tell this system otherwise avoids. Update 2026-09-29: that second half now exists — both overlays close with data-closing exit choreography (modal 200ms recede, drawer 220ms slide), live-verified in the running app. Everything that animates is purposeful; nothing pulses, nothing bounces, nothing staggers by default.

ISSUES TO ADDRESS
Critical Emil Jakub

Registration modal vanishes on close — no exit state Applied

WhatThe registration modal (and all np-modal consumers: sponsor, superadmin management) animates in with a 300ms blur-free rise at scale 0.972, then unmounts in a single frame on Cancel, X, or success. The backdrop cut is doubled by a blur removal snap.

Why it mattersExits are the half of choreography that tells users "this is gone, and here's where focus returns." A hard cut on the most consequential form in the product (27 fields) reads as a crash at the exact moment users are most invested. This is the audit checklist's own Critical: "No exit animations (elements just disappear)."

Recommended motionExit must be subtler than the enter: backdrop fades 300→0 over 200ms ease while the panel recedes upward (translateY(-6px) scale(0.99), 200ms expo-out). Drive it with a [data-closing] attribute and unmount after the 200ms window. Keyboard Escape and the success path skip the exit entirely — keyboard-initiated and completed-task dismissal should be instant.

Critical Emil Jakub

Mobile drawer and its backdrop snap out of existence Applied

WhatThe mobile navigation drawer mounts instantly and closes by removing both elements from the DOM in one frame — backdrop and panel both. The same file's desktop rail, by contrast, animates its width with a 300ms ease-in-out, so the mobile experience is measurably less considered than the desktop one.

Why it mattersThis is primary UI on phones — the drawer is how every mobile user reaches Logout, Profile, and Settings. Motion gaps in primary UI are a Critical on the audit checklist, and the contrast with the polished desktop rail makes the snap feel broken rather than minimal.

Recommended motionEnter from the edge it lives on: panel slides translateX(-24px)→0 at 0.4→1 opacity over 300ms expo-out while the backdrop fades in. Exit subtler: translateX(-16px) fade over 220ms, backdrop 200ms. Release the body scroll lock at close-request time so the page is interactive while the drawer recedes, and cancel an in-flight close if the user re-opens mid-exit (the forwards fill would otherwise hold it invisible).
